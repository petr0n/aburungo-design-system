What changed underneath

The tags are all readable now. Rokushō's label was warm paper on Rokushō 500 — 3.19:1, a failing AA pair shipping in PhraseCard since the accent set was built, and never gated. It is stone-900 now at 5.46:1. Ōgon already did exactly this, a dark label on a light accent.

All four accent pairs are gated as of this change. They were not before, and the four hues are nowhere near the same lightness, so passing on one said nothing about the others.

The glass is the same utility as the empty state — 36→16 gradient, 4px blur, 2px paper rim, the lift shadow. Padding is tightened for a list row; a lesson item is not a hero panel. It keeps backdrop-filter, which is load-bearing: it smooths the crest before compositing, and text measures worse without it.
