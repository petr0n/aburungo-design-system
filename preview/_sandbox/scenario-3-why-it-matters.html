Why the tag numbers matter when nothing looks wrong

You asked whether you were supposed to see something on the last page. Honestly: no. On a good screen with full sight, a white pill on gold and a white pill on green both look fine — which is exactly why the measurement exists. A contrast ratio is not a prediction about your monitor; it predicts whether the thing survives the conditions the product is actually used in.
