What to look for. 2 → 4 adds no colour, no shadow, no ornament and no asset. Every change is material: tooth, edge, impression, and giving the kana the room it was being denied. That is the distinction the Material section now records — restraint as precision rather than as subtraction. If 4 still reads as too quiet, the next move is illustration, which is a drawing problem and not a dial problem.

What the lab turned up. Building it surfaced a token gap nobody had noticed: the JP ramp stops at --text-jp-display-lg (40px), documented as JP at tablet+ and sized for a phrase. A single kana that is the task object gets the same size as a full sentence. Column 4 proposes --text-jp-glyph at 52px, continuing the ramp's own progression. That is a one-line addition to src/tokens.css and it is independent of whether the material treatment wins.
