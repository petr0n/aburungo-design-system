Focus ring — keeping the gold

Ōgon 500 is the ring the author chose and kept. It does not reach the 3:1 that WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.11 asks of a non-text indicator on any ground it lands on. Option B is the route to having both: the criterion measures the indicator against the colour that abuts it, so adding a 1px dark outer edge means the page is judged against the hairline rather than against the gold. The band still reads gold.
