Crest tile size — no new pattern needed

The crest size is a variable, not a property of the artwork: --tile-size, already exposed as .tile-sm / .tile-md / .tile-lg. You are currently seeing 100px. Below are four sizes on the chosen glass-card treatment.

The reason these are measured rather than just shown: brand.css records the glass as sitting 0.39 over the contrast bar and warns that a denser crest breaks it. Smaller tile means more crest edges per card, so the composite under the panel darkens. Each column's number is the meta line — the lightest text on the card, and the first thing that fails.
