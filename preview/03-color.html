Warm stone — the neutrals
The warmth is load-bearing. Against cool grey, Ai-iro reads as a tint of the surface; against warm paper it reads as ink on a page. Cards (0) sit lighter than the page (50) so they lift without a shadow — #FFFDF8 is not a mistake for #FFFFFF.
