Why darkening the progress track makes contrast worse

Contrast is how different two colours are in brightness. The green fill is medium brightness. The track is already lighter than it — so making the track darker moves it towards the green, not away, and the bar gets harder to read. It only improves again once the track is darker than the green itself.

Contrast at each track brightness

Why there is no fix inside the palette. Every darker step — stone-200, stone-300 — makes it worse, and stone-400 is worst of all because it is the closest in brightness to the green. Only two steps clear 3:1. stone-0 is the card colour, so the empty part of the bar would disappear into the card behind it. stone-700 and darker read as a header band, not an input well. Changing the fill would work — Rokushō 600 gives 3.87:1 — but that means moving off the locked 500 step, which is a palette decision, not a component one.
